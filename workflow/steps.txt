## 🔄 PharmaGraph End-to-End Pipeline

The complete workflow and operational pipeline of **PharmaGraph** are structured across the following core stages:

1. **Data Ingestion & Preprocessing**  
   * Ingesting multi-source biomedical data from PrimeKG and handling temporal snapshots ($t_0$ and $t_1$).  
   * Standardizing nodes and directed edges using rigorous database accessions (`NCBI`, `DrugBank`, `MONDO`, `GO`, `UBERON`, etc.).

2. **Temporal Edge Dynamics & Splitting**  
   * Categorizing graph interactions into three distinct dynamic classes: **Persistent**, **Added**, and **Removed** edges to capture true structural evolution over time.

3. **Graph Machine Learning & Representation Learning**  
   * Training robust graph neural network architectures and vectorizing random walks (extending DREAMwalk and graph embedding models) for link prediction and drug repurposing.

4. **Scoring, Evidence Integration & Path Explanation**  
   * Computing multi-hop biological paths (Drug $\rightarrow$ Target $\rightarrow$ Pathway $\rightarrow$ Disease) and integrating auxiliary evidence features (PPI, GO similarity) for evidence-aware ranking.

5. **Interactive R Shiny Dashboard & Visualization**  
   * Exposing temporal graph snapshots, metrics (`statistics.json`), and 1-hop disease-specific networks through a modular, interactive R Shiny dashboard (`dashboard/` component).

6. **Rigorous Evaluation & Next Steps**  
   * Testing exclusively on the updated snapshot, tracking multi-task performance, and executing parallel workflows across coding, manuscript drafting, and presentation polish.
